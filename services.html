Before you ask

How much does a website cost?
It depends on the number of pages and features, so I quote each project separately after a short chat. The quote is fixed — it doesn't grow unless you add to the scope.
How long does it take?
A simple business website is usually live within one to two weeks. Web apps take longer; you'll get a timeline with the quote.
You're a student — will you have time?
I take on a small number of projects at a time so each one gets proper attention, and I'll tell you honestly if I can't meet your deadline before you pay anything.
Do I own the website?
Yes. The domain is registered in your name and you get the full source code. You're never locked in to me.
Do you work outside Kerala?
Yes — everything can be done remotely over email, calls and a live preview link.
