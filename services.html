Before you ask

How much does a website cost in Kerala?
With me, a small business website starts at ₹4,999 for up to 5 pages, including mobile-friendly design, a contact form or WhatsApp button, on-page SEO and Google Business Profile setup. Web apps start at ₹14,999 and fixes to an existing site start at ₹999. Every project gets a fixed quote before work begins.
What is included in the ₹4,999 website?
Up to 5 pages, design that works on phones, a contact form or WhatsApp button, basic SEO (titles, descriptions, structured data, sitemap), Google Business Profile setup, free hosting for static sites, and 30 days of bug fixes after launch. The domain name is extra, about ₹800–₹1,200 a year, registered in your name.
Are there monthly fees?
Not for a standard business website. Static sites are hosted free, so the only recurring cost is the yearly domain renewal. Web apps that need a server or database may have a hosting cost, which I'll state in the quote.
How long does it take to build a website?
A simple business website is usually live within 1–2 weeks. Web apps take longer; you'll get a timeline with the quote.
You're a student — will you have time?
I take on a small number of projects at a time so each one gets proper attention, and I'll tell you honestly if I can't meet your deadline before you pay anything.
Do you work outside Kerala?
Yes — anywhere in India or abroad, remotely over email, calls and a live preview link. In Kerala I can also meet in person around Malappuram and Thrissur.
Do I own the website?
Yes. The domain is registered in your name and you get the full source code. There's no lock-in.
